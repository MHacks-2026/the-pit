# Real-data tournament and Cop check

Generated by `packages/bots/src/realDataEval.test.ts`. 171 held-out real price paths (BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD, LTC-USD 1-minute candles from Coinbase, 2026-09-04 to 2026-10-04, rescaled to HACK ticks; 1 real minute = 1 simulated second), 299 s each. These are the paths the adaptive AI never trained on. Every session runs the real matching engine with the market maker, three noise traders, the informed trader (which sees the hidden value) and the trained adaptive AI (which does not); the Cop replays each session's event log once a second. Simulated traders, play money: real data shapes only the hidden value.

## How the Cop does on real-data markets

| Condition | Sessions | Spoofer caught | Spoofer trades | Alerts on spoofer | Alerts on other accounts | Sessions with a false alarm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No spoofer | 171 | n/a | n/a | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Spoofer (default: trade 1 s after layering, cancel 1 s after) | 171 | 171/171 | 4374 | 2266 | 0 | 0 |
| Evasive spoofer: trades 4 s after layering | 171 | 0/171 | 3880 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Evasive spoofer: cancels 6 s after trading | 171 | 0/171 | 3638 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

No account other than the spoofer was ever flagged, including the market maker and the adaptive AI.

Profit is mark-to-market in play dollars per session. "Spoofer trades" counts its opposite-side fills (one per completed cycle, sometimes more). The evasive rows step outside the Cop's 3 s / 5 s windows on purpose; see docs/LIMITATIONS.md.
