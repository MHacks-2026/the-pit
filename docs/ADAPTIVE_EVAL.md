# Adaptive AI trader: held-out evaluation

Generated by `packages/bots/src/adaptiveTraining.test.ts`. Priors were learned on 133 real price paths plus 50 synthetic sessions; these results are on 55 **held-out** real paths (299 s each). Data: BTC-USD, ETH-USD, SOL-USD, LTC-USD 1-minute candles from Coinbase, 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-04, rescaled to HACK ticks (see packages/bots/data/pricePaths.json).

Profit is mark-to-market in play dollars per session. The AI sees only public information (book, recent mids, its own position).
